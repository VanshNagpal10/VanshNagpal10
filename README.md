## Research

### From Glass Box to Fair Box: A Causal Explainability Framework for Ethical AI Hiring Systems
*Co-authored with Jayati Ahuja*

Proposes **CEFHF** — a six-layer causal pipeline that exposes fairness violations in AI-driven hiring systems. The central argument: *interpretability ≠ fairness*. SHAP and LIME explain correlations; they are blind to proxy discrimination via backdoor causal paths. Introduces the **Counterfactual Fairness Violation Rate (CFVR)** metric, derives formal theoretical bounds on fairness under partial SCM specification, and characterises the Pareto-optimal fairness-accuracy trade-off under causal constraint regularisation.

Validated against SHAP and LIME baselines on the **Utrecht Fairness Recruitment Dataset**.

> *"A model that rejects candidates from zip codes correlated with race will produce perfectly legible SHAP attributions — while committing clear discrimination."*

`Explainable AI` · `Causal Fairness` · `Structural Causal Models` · `Counterfactual Fairness` · `Algorithmic Hiring` · `SHAP` · `LIME` · `DECAF` · `Human-in-the-Loop`
